FAQ

Do invoices go out in my name?

Yes. Invoices use your business name and approved rates, and you keep the customer relationship.

What do I need to share?

Your job records or tow tickets, your rate sheets and contracts, limited access to send invoices, and one contact who can answer questions about disputed jobs.

How does the free trial work?

You send 5 recent jobs and your rate sheet. I prepare draft invoices for you to review, and nothing is sent to your customers.

How much does it cost?

After the trial I share simple monthly pricing. It’s month-to-month, so you can stop at the end of any month.
